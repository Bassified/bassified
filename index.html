What does Bassified mean?

Bass is the foundation of every song. You don’t always notice it, but without it everything sounds thin. “-ified” means “made into”, so Bassified means giving your brand a solid foundation: depth, weight and a groove people feel.

It’s a name I made up, the way Google and Spotify did, and it comes from who I am: a full-time bass player who designs. I build the base first, then everything above it works.
